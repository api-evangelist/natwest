# NatWest Group — Bank of APIs (Open Banking)

> NatWest Group's "Bank of APIs" developer platform publishes the UK Open Banking
> (CMA9 / PSD2) APIs implemented to the OBIE Read/Write API Standard v3.1.11:
> Account & Transaction Information (AISP), Payment Initiation (PISP), and
> Confirmation of Funds (CBPII). Access is FAPI 1.0 Advanced OAuth2/OIDC with PSD2
> strong customer authentication, mutual-TLS client authentication, dynamic client
> registration, and OBIE/eIDAS certificates. A self-service sandbox is available.

## APIs

- [Account and Transaction API](https://www.bankofapis.com/products/natwest-group-open-banking/accounts/documentation/nwb): AISP — accounts, balances, transactions, beneficiaries, standing orders, direct debits, statements (accounts scope).
- [Payment Initiation API](https://www.bankofapis.com/products/natwest-group-open-banking/payments/documentation/nwb): PISP — domestic, scheduled, international, standing-order, and file payments (payments scope).
- [Confirmation of Funds API](https://www.bankofapis.com/products/natwest-group-open-banking/accounts/documentation/nwb): CBPII — funds-confirmation consents and checks (fundsconfirmations scope).
